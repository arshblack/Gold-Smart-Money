# Methodology Overview

This project studies structured gold-market behavior using high-level concepts such as market context, liquidity behavior, session timing, activity confirmation, and multi-timeframe alignment.

The public methodology is intentionally general. It does not publish exact signal rules, thresholds, formulas, or execution conditions.

## High-Level Research Themes

- Higher-timeframe context with lower-timeframe execution review
- Session-based market observation
- Liquidity and market-structure analysis
- Feed-specific activity and relative-volume context
- Setup lifecycle tracking, including invalidation and expiry
- Risk discipline and controlled exposure
- Dashboard-driven review and Telegram alert workflows
- Backtest, demo forward-test, and out-of-sample review

## What Is Not Shared

TradingView research states are separated from EA-confirmed trades. The exact model logic, scoring weights, execution rules, risk formulas, and signal thresholds remain private.
